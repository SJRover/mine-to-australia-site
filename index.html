Mine to Australia: Support

A game about digging a hole in your back garden, all the way to Australia.

Getting help

If something isn't working, or you have a question or an idea, email sjrovers1995@gmail.com. It helps to say which device you're playing on and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Where is my game saved?

Your progress is saved on your iPhone or iPad as you play. Deleting the app deletes your saved games, so keep it installed if you want to carry on later. Updates to the game keep your progress.

Moving to a new device

There is no online account, so your game moves to a new iPhone or iPad when you set it up from a backup of your old one.

Privacy

The game collects no personal information at all. You can read the full privacy policy.
