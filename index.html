Mine to Australia

Support

Mine to Australia is a game for iPhone and iPad about digging a hole in your back garden, through the Earth and all the way to Australia. This page explains how to get help with it.

Contact us

If something is not working as it should, or you have a question or a suggestion, please email sjrovers1995@gmail.com. It helps us to know which device and version of iOS or iPadOS you are using, and what you were doing when the problem occurred.

Saved games

Your progress is saved automatically on your device as you play. Updates to the app keep your progress, but deleting the app permanently deletes your saved games, so please keep it installed if you would like to continue later.

Moving to a new device

The app has no online account, so your saved games move to a new iPhone or iPad when you set it up from a backup of your previous device.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data. Our privacy policy explains this in full.
