Mine to Australia

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains how Mine to Australia (“the app”) handles information. The app does not collect any personal data.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. It has no user accounts or sign-in, contains no advertising, and does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. No information about you or about how you use the app is sent to us or to any third party. This applies to every user of the app, including children.

Game data stored on your device

Your saved games and settings are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad, within the app’s own storage. This data never leaves your device and cannot be accessed by us. Deleting the app permanently deletes it. If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, this data may be included in those backups, which are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

In-app purchases

The current version of the app offers no in-app purchases. If a future version offers them, every transaction will be processed by Apple through the App Store. We will not receive your payment details or any other personal information; the app will only confirm with the App Store whether an item has been purchased.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if a new feature changes how the app handles information. Any change will be published on this page with a revised effective date before it takes effect in the app.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about privacy in the app, please contact us at sjrovers1995@gmail.com.
