Mine to Australia: Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Mine to Australia does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, so nothing about you or how you play is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Your saved games

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, your saved games are deleted with it. If you back up your device, Apple may include the game's saves in that backup, under Apple's own privacy policy, which means restoring the backup can bring your game back.

Purchases

This version of the game has no in-app purchases. If a later version offers one, the purchase will be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We will never receive your payment details or any personal information, only whether the item is owned on your device.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If anything here ever changes, for example because a new feature needs it, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
